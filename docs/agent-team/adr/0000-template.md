# ADR NNNN: <short title in the imperative>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** product owner, architect
- **Ticket:** #N (requirement ID)

## Context

What problem or need forces a decision? Constraints, existing structure, relevant facts. Keep it short and concrete.

## Options considered

1. **Option A:** what it is, pros, cons.
2. **Option B:** what it is, pros, cons.
3. **Do nothing** (when relevant).

## Decision

The chosen option, in one or two sentences, and the main reason.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what gets better.
- **Negative / costs:** what gets worse, what we accept.
- **Impact on tests:** what becomes easier or harder to test; new test types needed.
- **Impact on operations:** deployment, configuration, monitoring, security.
- **Follow-ups:** technical tickets or later reviews.

## When to revisit

The condition or date that would make us reconsider this decision.
